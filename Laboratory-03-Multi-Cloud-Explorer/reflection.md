# Mission Reflection

Completing this Multi-Cloud Explorer mission gave me a much clearer picture of how the three major cloud providers compare in practice, rather than just in theory.

**1. Which cloud platform impressed you the most?**
Google Cloud Platform impressed me the most, mainly because of how purpose-built its AI/ML and Kubernetes tooling is. Knowing that Google created Kubernetes and backs it with services like Vertex AI and custom TPUs made it clear why GCP is often the go-to choice for data-heavy and AI-focused organizations, even though AWS and Azure remain more dominant overall.

**2. What similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP?**
Despite their differences, all three platforms share the same fundamental building blocks: virtual machines for compute, object storage services, managed relational databases, identity and access management systems, and a global network of regions/zones for redundancy and low latency. They also all follow a pay-as-you-go pricing model and offer web-based management consoles alongside CLI tools for automation.

**3. How do business requirements influence cloud platform selection?**
This mission made it obvious that there's no single "best" cloud provider — the right choice depends entirely on the client's existing environment, technical needs, and budget. A university already using Microsoft tools benefits most from Azure's integration, while an AI research company gets more value from GCP's machine learning infrastructure. Choosing a platform is about solving the client's specific problem, not picking whichever platform is most popular.

**4. What did you learn from acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant?**
Acting as a consultant taught me to evaluate technology from a business perspective first, and a technical perspective second. Instead of asking "which cloud is best," I had to ask "what does this specific client actually need," which is a more realistic way to approach real-world cloud architecture decisions.

**5. How has your GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio improved after completing this mission?**
My portfolio now includes structured, well-documented comparisons and recommendations across all three major cloud providers, backed by real research, screenshots, and hands-on Linux investigation — making it a stronger demonstration of practical cloud evaluation skills.
